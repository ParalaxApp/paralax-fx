Drag it on

Find it under Effects, in Titles or Effects. Drop a title above your clip, or an effect onto it.

Questions

Before you buy

Can I use these in monetised videos?

Yes. Use them in as many of your own videos as you like, including monetised and client work. You just can't resell or share the template files themselves.

Which kit do I need?

If you use the free version of DaVinci Resolve, get HUD Kit. If you own Resolve Studio, HUD Kit Studio adds the AI depth effects on top.

Do I need to know Fusion?

No. Titles and effects are drag and drop with controls in the Inspector. The two 3D scenes are pasted onto the Fusion page, and the included guide shows you where to plug your clips in.

Will it slow my timeline down?

The footage effects are heavier than plain titles. If playback stutters, use Render Cache or Render in Place while you edit.
