Licence

When you buy a Paralax FX kit, you get a licence to use it. You don't buy the template files themselves. Here's what that means in plain English.

You can

  • Use the kit in as many of your own projects as you like, including monetised YouTube videos, streams, ads and paid client work.
  • Install it on every computer you personally use.
  • Change the templates however you like for your own projects.
  • Keep everything you render. Your finished videos are yours.

You can't

  • Sell, share, give away or upload the template files, whether changed or not.
  • Put the templates, or parts of them, into another template pack, preset bundle or stock library.
  • Share one licence between several people. Each editor needs their own.

Updates and support

Updates to a kit you own are free. If something doesn't work in your version of DaVinci Resolve, email fx@paralaxverse.com and we'll help.
