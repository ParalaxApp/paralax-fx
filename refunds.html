Refunds

Kits are digital downloads. When you check out, you agree to get the download straight away, which means the usual 14-day right to cancel no longer applies once your download starts.

If a kit doesn't work in your copy of DaVinci Resolve and we can't fix it for you, you get a full refund. Email [support email] within 14 days of buying with your order number and what went wrong.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Lemon Squeezy. They usually show within 5 to 10 working days.
