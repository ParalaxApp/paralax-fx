Privacy

This site is a shop window. It has no accounts, no cookies of its own and no tracking or analytics.

When you buy

Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record. They collect what's needed to take payment and deliver your download, such as your name, email address and payment details, and handle it under their own privacy policy. We see your name, email and order details so we can support you. We never see your card details.

Emails

We only email you about your order, and about kit updates if you opt in at checkout. You can unsubscribe from any update email.

Fonts and videos

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when you visit. Demo videos are served from this site.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email [support email].
